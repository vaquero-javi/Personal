# Intro / pantalla de título

Scope: first screen of the birthday game (intro) plus the Level 1 placeholder it leads to. Mode: Experience. Audience: her, on phone or desktop. Action: press "jugar".

## Direction contract

THESIS: The gift is a game, so its first screen is a real arcade title screen starring her: a boot, a title, a dialogue box, PRESS START. Refuses the birthday-card default (confetti, balloons, script font, centered message over a soft photo).

OWN-WORLD: Her night-party photo as the cabinet's attract-mode plate under fine scanlines. Palette pulled from the photo: wristband hot pink, leopard/fairy-light gold, deep plum-black ink, paper white. Pixel faces only (chunky pixel display, readable pixel text face). Stepped pixel borders on the dialogue box, pixel-art SVG icons (heart lives, cursor arrow). No glows, no glass, no gradient text.

STORY: Screen powers on like a CRT, HUD and title "¡FELIZ CUMPLEAÑOS, MI VIDA!" drop in, a RPG dialogue box types the rest of his message; she understands it's a game with levels that unlock surprises, and presses JUGAR.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Photo full-bleed, her face kept in frame at all aspects. HUD strip at top (1UP, hearts, NIVEL 0). Title large across the upper-middle third on desktop / upper area on mobile. Dialogue box anchored bottom, max ~60ch, typed text with skip-on-tap. JUGAR is a big pixel button inside/under the dialogue box with a blinking cursor arrow; it appears once the text finishes (or immediately on skip / reduced motion).

FORM: Arcade title screen, my position 1 (pick card), seed key 1379a2fe. Signature interaction: CRT power-on + typewriter dialogue; JUGAR triggers a stepped pixel-wipe transition to the "NIVEL 1" screen.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite (user's choice). Static build, deployable to any static host.

## Purpose

A personal birthday gift: a small web game made by her partner. Each level she completes unlocks a surprise. The effort of playing is part of the gift ("esta vez no hay regalos sin esfuerzo").

## Users

One person: his girlfriend, on her birthday. She opens it on phone or desktop equally, so both must feel first-class.

## Content and assets

- Photos in `images/`: `isi0.jpg` intro background; `isi3.jpg` nivel suegros, `isi2.jpg` nivel cuñados, `isi1.jpg` nivel novio.
- Intro copy, verbatim from the author: "¡Feliz cumpleaños, mi vida! Esta vez no hay regalos sin esfuerzo... ¡he preparado un juego para ti! Conforme vayas completando cada nivel de la app, irás desbloqueando tus sorpresas. ¡Te amo con el alma, a jugar!"
- Primary action label: "jugar".

## Voice

Spanish (Spain), intimate, playful, affectionate. Written by him to her; never generic or corporate.

## Open decisions

- Three levels, in this fixed order: nivel suegros, nivel cuñados, nivel novio, chosen from an arcade-cabinet level select. Nivel suegros is a word search; its words are placeholders chosen by the builder, pending the author's list. Nivel cuñados is a Block Blast recreation won by reaching a target score (300, builder's choice, pending the author's number). Nivel novio is a Screw Out 3D recreation: a 3D house taken apart screw by screw (roof first, then what's under it), won by removing every screw in the house (45). Win text for both: "Ya puedes abrir sus regalos".
